Case studies

Superfoods brand

London

Context

A 40+ SKU portfolio across powders, blends, capsules, teas and multiple pack formats, supplied through 20+ co-manufacturers and co-packers to major retailers.

What I did

  • Issued a competitive RFQ for warehousing and distribution and led a thorough supplier selection process.
  • Negotiated and reviewed pricing across capsule production, pouch packing and tea components.
  • Introduced COGS tracking, production planners and cost timelines so leadership could see cost drivers early.
  • Built forecasting and planning models that turn sales data into raw-material and finished-goods requirements.

Result

Warehousing and distribution costs fell 40% year on year, with improved rates and no compromise on service levels. Documented procurement savings reached £36,000+, and supply held for major retail accounts through a period of pressure on stock and lead times.

CBD manufacturer

Manufacturing and retail, London

Context

A manufacturing and retail business where I led a team of around 10 across Core Ops, Production and Customer Excellence.

What I did

  • Devised and executed a new business strategy to reduce operating costs.
  • Renegotiated raw-material costs and ran a process review to remove inefficiency.
  • Renegotiated carrier rates and switched suppliers where needed.
  • Built stock management and forecasting systems, and managed new product development and entry into the US market.
  • Set accountability and collaborative targets across the teams.

Result

Operating costs fell by £202,000 in one year. In another year, raw-material renegotiation saved about £60,000 and the process review about £35,000. Logistics costs dropped 45% with no loss of service, average inventory value fell 33%, and team productivity rose from 61% to 86%.

Other roles

  • Luxury chocolate company, London. Led three teams totalling 23 people through peak season, owning logistics, warehousing, inventory, production and distribution.
  • Renewable energy company, Nigeria (subsidiary of Shell). Cut operating costs 30% and export costs 50%, and built ISO 9001:2015 processes for procurement, stock and fleet.
